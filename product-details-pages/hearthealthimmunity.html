What Is Heart Health & Immunity Booster?

Heart Health & Immunity Booster by Aiva Wellness is a daily dietary supplement that combines Co-Enzyme Q10 (CoQ10), Lycopene, Omega-3 fatty acids, Vitamin B12, Zinc and mixed carotenoids in one veg capsule. Each bottle contains 60 capsules, which is a 60-day routine at one capsule per day.

It is designed for adults who want everyday nutritional support for heart wellness, healthy circulation, cellular energy and immune resilience. New to these nutrients? Start with our CoQ10, Lycopene and Omega-3 heart health guide.

Key Ingredients Explained

Co-Enzyme Q10 (CoQ10)

CoQ10 is a nutrient the body uses in cellular energy production, which is why it is often discussed in the context of heart wellness. Read more in What Is CoQ10? Benefits and How to Take It.

Omega-3 Fatty Acids

Omega-3 fatty acids help support healthy blood flow and circulation as part of a balanced diet. Prefer a standalone option? See Aiva Omega-3 Fish Oil.

Lycopene & Mixed Carotenoids

Lycopene and mixed carotenoids are antioxidant nutrients that help protect cells from oxidative stress. Learn more in Lycopene and Carotenoids: Antioxidant Support Explained.

Vitamin B12 & Zinc

Vitamin B12 supports energy metabolism and Zinc supports immune function, rounding out the immunity side of the formula.

Who May Consider It?

Adults looking for a simple daily routine for heart and immune wellness alongside a balanced diet and active lifestyle. It is a supplement, not a replacement for food or prescribed care. Consult a healthcare professional if you take medication or manage a health condition.

How to Use It in Your Daily Routine

Take one capsule daily with water after meals, ideally at the same time each day. See the How to Use tab for full directions and the safety notes below.
