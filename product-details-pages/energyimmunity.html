What Is Energy & Immunity Multivitamin with Zinc?

Energy & Immunity by Aiva Wellness is a daily multivitamin, multimineral and zinc supplement in one veg capsule. It brings together Vitamin A, C, D3 and E, the B-complex vitamins, and minerals such as zinc, selenium, magnesium, copper and iodine. Each bottle holds 60 capsules, a 60-day routine at one capsule per day. For a step-by-step overview, read our complete guide to multivitamin, multimineral and zinc capsules.

Why Choose a Multivitamin, Multimineral & Zinc Capsule?

Busy routines and uneven diets can leave small nutritional gaps. A multivitamin with zinc brings many essential micronutrients together in one easy daily capsule, so you do not need to manage several separate bottles. It complements, but never replaces, a varied diet.

Energy Support: B-Vitamins for Daily Vitality

B-vitamins contribute to normal energy-yielding metabolism and help reduce tiredness and fatigue. Learn how they work in B-vitamins for energy: what they do and how to get enough.

Immune Support: Zinc, Vitamin C & Vitamin D3

Zinc, Vitamin C and Vitamin D3 each contribute to the normal function of the immune system. Together with other nutrients in the formula they help support everyday immune resilience. Read more in Zinc, Vitamin C and D3: immune support explained.

Antioxidant Support: Vitamin C & Vitamin E

Vitamins C and E are antioxidant nutrients that help protect cells from oxidative stress.

Multimineral Support for Everyday Nutrition

Essential minerals such as zinc, manganese, copper, selenium, chromium, magnesium, iodine and molybdenum round out the formula to help fill everyday nutritional gaps. Looking for a dedicated mineral formula? See Magnesium Complex 5-in-1. Prefer a broader adult formula? Compare with Daily Vitals for Adults 18+.

Who Can Take Energy & Immunity Multivitamin?

Adults looking for a simple daily routine for energy and immune wellness alongside a balanced diet and active lifestyle. It is a supplement, not a replacement for food or prescribed care. Consult a healthcare professional if you take medication or manage a health condition.

How to Take Energy & Immunity Capsules

Take one capsule daily with water after meals, ideally at the same time each day. Not sure when to take yours? Read the best time to take a multivitamin. See the How to Use tab for full directions and the safety notes below.
